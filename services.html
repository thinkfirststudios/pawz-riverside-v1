In-Home Pet Sitting

Your home is your pet's sanctuary, and that's exactly where they should stay. Our in-home pet sitting service brings a caring, experienced sitter right to your door so your dog or cat can stay in the comfort of their own environment, surrounded by familiar smells and their favorite spots.

Whether you need a single daily check-in or an overnight stay, we tailor every visit to your pet's specific needs and routine. We serve dogs and cats only, so you can trust that your fur baby is getting focused, species-appropriate attention every time.

New clients receive a complimentary meet-and-greet so your pet can get comfortable with us before the first booking.

What's Included

  • Overnight stays or daily check-in visits
  • Daily feeding on your pet's schedule
  • Fresh water every visit
  • Invigorating walks tailored to your dog's energy level
  • Cuddle time, play, and enrichment activities
  • Photo and text updates after every visit
  • Oral or topical medication administration if needed
  • Litter box cleaning for cats

What's Included

  • Door-to-door pickup from your home
  • Safe transport with carrier or seatbelt harness
  • Drop-off at your veterinarian's office
  • Pickup and return trip after the appointment
  • Text updates while en route and at the clinic
  • Calm, patient handling throughout the journey
  • Waiting at the clinic if needed (fee applies)
  • Post-appointment notes relayed to you

Pet Taxi

Life is busy, but your pet's health can't wait. Our pet taxi service takes the stress out of vet visits by handling every step of the journey, from doorstep pickup to safe drop-off and back home again.

We transport dogs and cats to and from veterinary offices, specialist clinics, and routine checkups throughout Riverside and Corona. Your pet is handled with calm, patient care the entire time, and you'll receive updates throughout so you always know where they are and how they're doing.

It's the missing piece that turns good pet care into truly complete pet care.
