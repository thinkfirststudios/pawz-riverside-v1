The Person Behind the Paws

About Pawz Riverside

A lifelong animal lover serving Riverside and Corona with heart, experience, and genuine care.

Hi, I'm Gigi

I've been a proud Riverside resident my whole life, and animals have been by my side every step of the way. From rescuing strays as a kid to fostering dogs and cats as an adult, caring for animals isn't just something I do. It's who I am.

After years of helping friends, neighbors, and colleagues care for their pets, I noticed a gap in our community: busy professionals, frequent travelers, and individuals with limited mobility needed a trustworthy partner who could step in and maintain their pet's routine without the stress of boarding or the unpredictability of large pet-sitting apps.

So I built Pawz Riverside around a simple idea. Every pet deserves to feel safe, cared for, and completely at home. And every pet parent deserves the kind of peace of mind that only comes from knowing their fur baby is in genuinely good hands.

Why I Started Pawz Riverside

"A few years ago, a neighbor of mine had a sudden family emergency out of state. She had two cats she adored, no one nearby to help, and was absolutely heartbroken at the thought of leaving them in a boarding facility they had never seen. I stepped in and cared for her cats every day she was gone. When she came home and saw how calm and happy they were, she burst into tears of relief.

That moment stayed with me. I thought: every pet owner deserves that peace of mind. Not just people lucky enough to know someone like me. Everyone. That's when I decided to make this official. Pawz Riverside exists so that no pet parent ever has to choose between their life and their pet's wellbeing."

Gigi, Founder of Pawz Riverside
Our Approach

Personalized Care, Not Batch Processing

Unlike standard boarding facilities or large-scale apps, Pawz Riverside offers a highly personalized, one-on-one service model. Our in-home care reduces pet anxiety, and our specialized pet taxi service creates a comprehensive care loop most independent sitters simply don't provide.
