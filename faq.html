Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services. Don't see your question? Reach out and we'll answer it personally.

We work with dogs and cats only. This focus allows us to provide expert, species-appropriate care rather than spreading thin across many different types of animals. If you have a dog, a cat, or both, you're in the right place.

We serve Riverside and Corona, California. If you're not sure whether we cover your specific neighborhood, reach out and we'll let you know.

We come to your home, so your pet never has to go anywhere unfamiliar. Depending on the plan you choose, we can do a 30-minute check-in, a 60-minute extended visit, or an overnight stay. Every visit includes feeding, fresh water, and personalized attention. Dogs get walks, cats get playtime and litter box care, and you get photo updates so you always know your pet is happy.

Our pet taxi service handles transportation to and from veterinary appointments when you can't make it yourself. We pick your pet up from your home, transport them safely to the vet using a carrier or seatbelt harness, and return them home after the appointment. You'll receive text updates throughout the trip so you always know where they are. We offer both one-way trips and full round trips.

Absolutely, and we strongly encourage it. Before your pet's first visit, we'll schedule a complimentary meet-and-greet at your home. This gives your pet a chance to get comfortable with us in their own environment, and gives us a chance to learn their routine, personality, dietary needs, and any quirks we should know about. It's the foundation for a great relationship.

Yes. Pawz Riverside is fully insured for pet care services. This means you're protected if something unexpected happens during a visit or transport. We take our responsibility to your pet and your home seriously.

Yes. We can administer oral medications (pills or liquids) and topical treatments as part of our pet sitting visits at no additional charge. Just provide clear instructions during the meet-and-greet and show us the correct dosage. For anything more complex, like injections, please contact us beforehand so we can discuss whether it's within our scope.
